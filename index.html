I'm Joedickson "Joe" Siega, a Power Platform Developer and Automation Engineer at Fujitsu Global Delivery Center with 4+ years building scalable business applications on PowerApps, Power Automate and VBA. I'm certified in Microsoft Power Platform Fundamentals (PL-900).
